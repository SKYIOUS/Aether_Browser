## Flags for the next slices

1. **D2-A COMPLETE:** `fetch_full_mock` was 2.28 s because `normalize_url`/`resolve_url` mangled `mock://` URLs into `https://mock://...` which missed mocks and hit real DNS timeouts (~270 ms × 10 lookups). Fixed: both functions pass through any URL containing `://`. Corrected baseline: ~10.5 ms (undelayed), ~109 ms (9 × 5 ms serial delays + pipeline overhead).

2. **Parsing regressions** in HTML/CSS parsing (parse_html +262%, parse_css_2k_rules +133%) are unrelated to D2-A and need separate investigation.

3. **Layout remains the dominant cost** at scale: `taffy_5k_wrapped` at 1.8 s dwarfs everything else. A2 culling does not help layout itself; profiling needed per D2-B.

4. **C3 CSP redirect revalidation** already lands (C3 complete). Any new D0/D1 work must not regress the post-redirect CSP recheck.
